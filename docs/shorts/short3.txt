You took a burst of photos, all of the same moment. Now you have to find the one that's actually sharp.

SlimSort spots these series automatically.

It puts them side by side and marks the sharpest photo for you.

Zoom into one, and all of them zoom together. So you can check every detail at once.

Pick your favorite with one key, and press enter. The rest goes into a trash folder, so nothing is lost by accident.

SlimSort is completely free for Windows and Mac. The link is in my profile.
