Stop dragging your photos into folders one by one. There's a much faster way.

In SlimSort, every folder gets its own number key.

You see a photo, you press a key, and it flies straight into that folder. One for family, two for holiday, three for nature.

Don't want to keep a photo? Just press space to skip it. The next one is already waiting.

Once you get into the rhythm, you can sort about a thousand photos in ten minutes.

Made a mistake? One shortcut brings the photo back, and nothing is ever overwritten.

SlimSort is completely free for Windows and Mac. The link is in my profile.
