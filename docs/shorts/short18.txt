Five Windows shortcuts that feel like cheat codes.

Windows plus V. Your clipboard history. Everything you copied, not just the last thing. The first time, just turn it on.

Windows plus Shift plus S. A screenshot of just the part you need.

Windows plus period. Emojis, anywhere you type.

Control plus Shift plus T. Reopens the browser tab you just closed.

And Windows plus Control plus D. A brand new, empty desktop. Which one did you not know? Follow for more.
