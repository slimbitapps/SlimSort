Your RAM might be running way slower than what you paid for.

Check it. Open Task Manager with Control, Shift, Escape.

Go to Performance, then Memory, and look at the speed.

Bought thirty-two hundred, but it says twenty-one thirty-three? Then XMP is off.

Turn on XMP, or EXPO on AMD, in your BIOS. One switch, and you get the speed on the box.

Check your motherboard manual first. Follow for more gaming tips.
