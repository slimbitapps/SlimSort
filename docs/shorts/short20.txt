How does your keyboard know which key you pressed?

Under the keys, there's a grid of wires. Rows, and columns.

Every key sits where one row crosses one column.

A tiny chip checks the grid, row by row, hundreds of times every second.

When a row and a column connect, it knows. That's the letter G.

That's why some keyboards get confused when you press lots of keys at once. Follow for more tech facts.
