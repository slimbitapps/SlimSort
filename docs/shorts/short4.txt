Three photo tricks for Windows that almost nobody uses. The last one saves me hours.

Number one. Select all your photos, press F2 and type a name. Windows renames every single one of them in order.

Number two. Type kind colon picture into the search bar of your drive, and you see every photo on your computer at once.

Number three. To sort them into folders, I use a free app called SlimSort. Every folder gets a number key, and the photo flies straight in.

Save this for your next photo cleanup. The link to the app is in my profile.
