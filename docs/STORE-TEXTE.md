## 🇬🇧 English

### Name
SlimSort – Photo Sorter

### Short description (Kurzbeschreibung)
Sort thousands of photos into folders with one key press per picture. Fast, safe, free – no ads, no account, no cloud.

### Description (Beschreibung)
```
Thousands of photos in one folder? SlimSort turns sorting them into a quick job.

Give each target folder a key – 1 for Family, 2 for Holiday, 3 for Nature – and press it for every photo. The photo moves, the next one appears instantly. That's it. Most people sort a thousand photos in about ten minutes.

SAFE BY DESIGN
• Never overwrites anything – duplicate names get a number
• Unlimited undo – every move can be reversed with Ctrl+Z
• Copies are verified before the original is removed
• Works with NAS and network drives: if the drive disappears, the photo simply stays where it is

FINDS YOUR BURST SHOTS
SlimSort detects series of similar photos taken seconds apart and shows them side by side. Zoom into all of them at once, see which one is the sharpest, keep your favorite and clear out the rest – in one go.

MADE FOR REAL LIBRARIES
• Tens of thousands of photos stay smooth
• JPG, PNG, HEIC, RAW previews (via Windows) and videos
• Live Photos and sidecar files (.xmp, .aae) move along automatically
• Several source folders at once
• Simple mode for beginners, Expert mode for power users
• Light and dark theme, English, German, French, Spanish and Italian

PRIVATE
No account, no ads, no tracking, no cloud. SlimSort works completely offline – your photos never leave your computer.

SlimSort is free. It's made by SlimBit Apps – small apps that do more and use less.
```
