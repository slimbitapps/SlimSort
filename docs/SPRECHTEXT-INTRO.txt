Let's be honest. You probably have thousands of photos sitting in one big folder, and you keep telling yourself you'll sort them someday.

Doing it by hand means dragging every single photo into the right folder, scrolling down, and doing it all over again, for hours.

That's exactly why I built SlimSort, the fastest way to sort your photos.

You just pick the folder with your photos, create a few target folders, and each of them gets its own key.

Then you simply press a key, and the photo moves into that folder. One for family, two for holiday, and space if you want to skip one. The next photo is already waiting for you.

Once you get into the rhythm, it gets really fast. You can sort about a thousand photos in roughly ten minutes.

And you don't have to worry about losing anything. Every step can be undone, files are never overwritten, and if your network drive goes offline, your photos simply stay where they are.

If you took ten shots of the same moment, SlimSort finds that series for you, shows the photos side by side, and suggests the sharpest one. You keep your favorite and clear out the rest in one go.

There's also an overview mode, support for multiple folders, a light and a dark design, and five languages. It's simple when you start, and powerful when you need it.

And the best part is that it's completely free. There are no ads, no account, and no cloud, so your photos never leave your computer.
