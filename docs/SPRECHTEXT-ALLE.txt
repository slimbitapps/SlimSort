Why does 144 frames per second feel so much smoother than 60?

At 60, a new picture shows up every 16.7 milliseconds.

At 144, every 6.9 milliseconds. More than twice as often.

So moving things jump less between frames. Your aim feels smoother, and the picture reacts faster.
